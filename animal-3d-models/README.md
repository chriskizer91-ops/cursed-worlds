# Animal 3D models

This is where the 3D animals for the games live, each kind of animal in its own folder.

- `zebu-cattle/` is for the Zebu cow herd at the ranch in Bardwell, Texas.

Some of the models and the way they are built will come from the other repos (`envoi-on-the-longest-night`
and `what_the_map_forgot`). Those repos stay as they are; anything copied from them says where it came from.
