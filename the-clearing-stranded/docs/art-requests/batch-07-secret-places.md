# Batch 7: the three secret places (3 pictures)

Dagr's secret places are in the game now: the **Bluff Cave** and the **Beaver Pond**, found by exploring the Creek
Bottom, and the **Salt Lick**, found by exploring the Prairie. Until their paintings come back, the game draws them
in its own pixel style, and the hunting and fishing games there borrow the Prairie and Pond paintings.

**How to use this:** the same as batch 1. Paste each prompt (the quoted block) into your image tool with
`01-camp.png` attached as the style reference, save each picture with the exact file name given, zip them, and send
the zip back. It helps to make each one two to four times and keep the one that follows the layout best.

As before, positions are *percent across* from the left edge and *percent down* from the top edge, and each spot is
where the thing stands on the ground. The game's walkable ground and the things you tap are already laid out from
these numbers.
