# Batch 2: the survivor and the partner (20 pictures, all Must)

**How to use this:** paste each prompt (the quoted block) into your image tool. Attach the picture named in
**[match style: file]** as the style reference, and the one in **[also attach: file]** as well, for the person's
look. Make **20** first; once you like it, every later sheet attaches it so the survivor looks the same everywhere.
Save each picture with the exact file name given, zip them, and send the zip back. High resolution is fine; I'll
compress everything.

The figures go on flat light gray (#D9D9D9) unless your tool can make a real transparent background. Draw them
large; the game shrinks them to about 80 pixels tall on a 1536-wide place.

**Prefer a woman?** For the survivor, swap the first sentence of the survivor paragraph in every prompt here for:
*"The survivor: a woman in her mid-thirties, lean and capable, with sun-tanned skin and brown hair in a short braid
under the cap."* For the partner, swap the first sentence of the partner paragraph for: *"The partner: a woman in her
early thirties, sturdy and broad-shouldered, with sun-tanned skin and short black hair under the cap."* Everything
else stays the same. Use the same choice in every prompt of this batch.
